# DeskInTab — voiceover draft

Approximately 226 words. Aim for a warm, conversational read, with pauses for the visible results. The planned film is two minutes; the exact timing will follow the recorded voice. Pronounce WebMCP as “Web M C P.”

This is a script for the planned demo, not a claim that its footage has already been recorded. The fictional Harbor material and demonstrated effects must be verified before the final voice take.

---

What if your AI agent could work beside you, in a computer you both understand?

This is DeskInTab: a desktop in your browser, where you and your agent share the same apps and files.

Here’s a small product team planning its next release. The research is here, but someone still has to turn it into a decision.

I ask the agent to review the interviews and prepare a recommendation. Through WebMCP, it reads the project’s files and uses the apps’ capabilities directly.

It searches the notes, compares the requests in a spreadsheet, and builds a chart. The numbers stay in an editable workbook.

It also creates a searchable feedback explorer. I can filter a theme and open the original interview behind a finding.

Then it draws a delivery plan and writes a formatted release brief. The chart and diagram become part of the report, alongside the editable originals.

But one decision still belongs to me: should search ship now, or after offline reading?

I choose offline reading first. The agent reads my saved answer, revises the brief, and updates the tasks. The project keeps the results, the decision, and what comes next.

I can inspect the changes, keep editing, or export a PDF. The work stays in this browser, ready for the next session.

DeskInTab. One desktop. Shared work. Something you can pick up and continue.

---

## Recording notes

- Read it as if showing a friend something you built. Avoid a sales-announcer voice.
- Slightly emphasize “same apps and files,” “editable,” and “one decision still belongs to me.”
- Leave a short pause after the question about search. That is where the person answers in Projects.
- Record in a quiet room at a consistent microphone distance. An unprocessed WAV is ideal; use the best existing recording setup rather than buying equipment for this.
- Record two full takes and a few seconds of room tone. Keep the first and last words intact; final trimming happens in editing.
- The script deliberately does not claim a fresh agent has already resumed. If we later capture that genuinely, replace the continuation passage to match the verified footage.
